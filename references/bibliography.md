### Precursor Languages

4. **JOSS (JOHNNIAC Open Shop System)**
   - Title: JOSS
   - URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JOSS
   - Date: First implemented 1963 at RAND Corporation
   - Summary: One of the first interactive, time-sharing programming languages. Pioneered conversational user interfaces and influenced TELCOMP, STRINGCOMP, and MUMPS.

5. **TELCOMP**
   - Title: TELCOMP
   - URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TELCOMP
   - Date: Developed at BBN ~1964, first revenue October 1965
   - Summary: Interactive conversational language based on JOSS, developed by BBN after Cliff Shaw from RAND visited in 1964. First implemented on PDP-1, used for commercial time-sharing service.

6. **STRINGCOMP**
   - Title: STRINGCOMP
   - URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/STRINGCOMP
   - Date: Developed at BBN in 1967
   - Summary: One of three variants of JOSS II (along with TELCOMP and FILECOMP). Featured extended string handling capabilities and was a strong influence in the development of MUMPS.

7. **FILECOMP**
   - Title: FILECOMP
   - URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FILECOMP
   - Summary: Another BBN variant of JOSS, designed for text file processing and typesetting.
