### Precursor Languages

7. **JOSS (JOHNNIAC Open Shop System)**
   - Title: JOSS
   - URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JOSS
   - Date: First implemented 1963 at RAND Corporation
   - Summary: First interactive time-sharing language; direct precursor to TELCOMP and MUMPS.
   - **Pearl Status**: PRECURSOR PEARL

8. **TELCOMP**
   - Title: TELCOMP
   - URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TELCOMP
   - Date: Developed at BBN ~1964, first revenue October 1965
   - Summary: Commercial time-sharing adaptation of JOSS; influenced STRINGCOMP and MUMPS.
   - **Pearl Status**: PRECURSOR PEARL

9. **STRINGCOMP**
   - Title: STRINGCOMP
   - URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/STRINGCOMP
   - Date: Developed at BBN in 1967
   - Summary: JOSS II variant with extended string handling; strong influence on MUMPS database integration.
   - **Pearl Status**: PRECURSOR PEARL

10. **FILECOMP**
    - Title: FILECOMP
    - URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FILECOMP
    - Summary: JOSS II variant for text file processing and typesetting.
    - **Pearl Status**: PRECURSOR PEARL
